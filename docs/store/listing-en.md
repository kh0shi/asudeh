## Name

Asudeh: SMS without ads

## Short description (max 80 characters)

Hides promotional SMS. No internet access, and it never deletes a message.

## Full description

Tired of promotional texts? Asudeh is a full SMS app that sets ads aside on its own,
so your inbox only holds the messages you're waiting for. Its detection rules are
built for Iranian SMS.

**Hidden, not deleted**
Asudeh never deletes a message. Ads go to the Promotions folder and suspected scams
to the Scams folder. Look in whenever you like and bring any message back with one
tap. Messages you delete yourself go to "Deleted" first.

**Important messages are never lost**
One-time codes and bank messages always stay in the Inbox. A message from a mobile
number or one of your contacts is never automatically hidden as an ad. Every message
is saved before anything else happens to it.

**Scam warnings**
A message that claims to be from a bank or official body but comes from another
number or domain, or carries a look-alike link, is shown with a warning and its
links are disabled.

**Why is it here?**
Every hidden message comes with one plain sentence explaining why. If it's wrong,
choose "Always in Inbox" or "Block"; your own rules always win over automatic
detection.

**Real privacy**
Asudeh has no permission to use the internet at all; check the app's permission
list. All detection runs on your phone, no statistics are collected, and cloud
backup is off. Asudeh is open source (GPL-3.0).

**Everything you expect from an SMS app**
- Send and receive SMS and MMS, and pick the SIM on dual-SIM phones
- Search across all messages, with Persian and Latin digits
- Copy one-time codes and reply right from the notification
- Scheduled send
- Pin, archive and mute conversations
- One-tap "Unsubscribe with 11" for promotional lines (asks first; it may cost one regular text)
- Daily or weekly digest of hidden messages
- Backup to a file in a location you choose
- Persian (Jalali) calendar, light and dark themes, Persian and English interface
